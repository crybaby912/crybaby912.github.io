Design systems motion adaptations
Source: https://github.com/ruiqichenbiec/design-systems
Revision: 7a424c93b9be13cf835b91ea67495fec2ec0584e

Adapted for this Astro portfolio:
- lens/lab/design-system/internal/jelly-physics.js: bounded-timestep Spring
- lens/lab/design-system/tokens.css: glass surface and edge treatment
- overture/src/dynamics.js: iris, develop, FLIP and mechanical counter patterns

The portfolio uses a small native DOM/CSS adaptation. It does not bundle the
upstream GPU renderer, audio, example images, fonts or scroll interception.
